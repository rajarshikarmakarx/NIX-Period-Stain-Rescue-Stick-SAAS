# NIX & CO. — PRODUCT + WEBSITE BUILD SPEC
### NIX Period Stain Rescue Stick

**Project Type:** D2C FMCG / College Startup Competition Prototype  
**Build Goal:** Premium, believable, interactive startup website  
**Priority:** Visual quality → storytelling → UX → functional demo flows → scalability

---

# 01 — PRODUCT CONTEXT

## Brand

**NIX & CO.**

NIX is a portable pre-treatment stain rescue stick designed specifically for fresh menstrual stains.

### Core idea

Most stain-removal products are designed for the laundry room.

**NIX is designed for the moment before you get there.**

The product is meant to live in a handbag, college bag, work bag, gym bag, or travel pouch and be available when an unexpected period stain happens outside the home.

---

# 02 — THE WEBSITE'S JOB

The website is a **D2C ecommerce prototype**, but it is also a pitch/demo experience.

It needs to make a competition judge think:

> “This looks like a real consumer startup, not a college project.”

The website should communicate:

1. The embarrassment/inconvenience of unexpected period stains.
    
2. Why existing stain-removal solutions aren't optimized for this situation.
    
3. Why a portable stick format makes sense.
    
4. How NIX is used.
    
5. What the product costs.
    
6. How a customer buys it.
    
7. How NIX can create repeat purchases.
    
8. How rewards/referrals can drive retention.
    
9. How NIX could expand into an Emergency Kit ecosystem.
    
10. That the founders understand branding, D2C, retention, and product expansion.
    

---

# 03 — IMPORTANT PRODUCT CLAIMS

Use confident but realistic language.

### Approved positioning

- “Portable pre-treatment stick for fresh menstrual stains.”
    
- “Made for the moment a stain happens.”
    
- “Keep it with you. Treat the stain. Deal with it later.”
    
- “Designed for life outside the laundry room.”
    
- “Small enough to keep in your everyday bag.”
    

### DO NOT claim:

- 100% stain removal
    
- Instant stain removal
    
- Guaranteed stain removal
    
- Works on every fabric
    
- Removes every stain
    
- Clinically proven
    
- Dermatologically tested
    
- FDA approved
    
- Any scientific/medical claim that has not been provided
    

If a feature or specification is unknown, use placeholder copy rather than inventing facts.

# 06 — DESIGN DIRECTION

## Overall aesthetic

Think:

**Premium D2C startup × beauty/lifestyle brand × modern FMCG**

The website should have strong visual hierarchy, generous whitespace, sharp typography, subtle motion, and high-quality product imagery.

Do NOT make every section look like a different website.

Create a coherent design system.

---

## Visual principles

- Strong typography
    
- Large editorial headlines
    
- Clean layouts
    
- Product-focused compositions
    
- High-quality mockups/placeholders
    
- Subtle micro-interactions
    
- Smooth scrolling
    
- Tasteful animations
    
- Rounded but not excessively bubbly UI
    
- Strong CTA hierarchy
    
- Mobile-first responsive behavior
    

Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, floating blobs, unnecessary 3D effects, or “AI-generated SaaS landing page” aesthetics.

# 42 — FINAL BUILD INSTRUCTION

Build NIX as if it were a **real D2C consumer startup launching its first product**, not as a generic college-project website.

The experience should be:

**fast → premium → discreet → memorable → believable**

The visitor should understand:

> **There is a specific problem.**  
> **Existing solutions aren't designed around this moment.**  
> **NIX is a simple product built specifically for it.**  
> **It is easy to carry and easy to buy.**  
> **The business can generate repeat purchases.**  
> **The brand can expand beyond the first product.**

Do not overcomplicate the product.

**The product is simple. The website should make that simplicity feel intentional.**

Use placeholders wherever final product photography, pricing, formulation details, packaging information, or other founder-supplied information is missing.

Keep those placeholders easy to replace without restructuring the application.

---

# 47 — UI SHAPE LANGUAGE

Use a restrained corner-radius system.

Suggested:

- Small controls: `8px`
- Inputs: `10px`
- Cards: `12–16px`
- Large feature containers: `20–24px`
- Product imagery: potentially larger radius depending on composition

Do not make every element pill-shaped.

Pills should be reserved for:

- Tags
- Status indicators
- Small badges
- Compact controls

Buttons can use modest rounding rather than full pills.

---

# 48 — BORDERS & SURFACES

Prefer subtle borders over heavy shadows.

Example:

```
border: 1px solid rgba(74, 48, 50, 0.12)
```

Cards should generally sit naturally on the cream background.

Use slightly differentiated cream/cherry-tinted surfaces when needed.

Avoid creating dozens of visual layers.

The UI should breathe.

- Large expressive headlines
- Medium/semibold section headings

- Compact uppercase labels
- Comfortable line-height
